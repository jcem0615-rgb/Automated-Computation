Calculation rules

  1. Raw total = qty × unit cost; final price = raw total × (1 + markup ÷ 100).
  2. Materials subtotal shows both the raw sum and the marked-up sum.
  3. Labor = workers × daily rate × days, or the flat fee when one is given.
  4. Direct fees = mobilization + permits + equipment rental + any extra fees.
  5. Subtotal = materials + labor + direct fees.
  6. VAT = subtotal × 12%, only when VAT status is “12% VAT”.
  7. Grand total = subtotal + VAT. Nothing is rounded until it is displayed.
